# Open questions for Cody

Things the app is guessing at until Cody answers. Each one says what the app does now.

## Coffered ceilings

- **What "drop" means with the #9554 corner bead.** The "Drop from ceiling" box needs one
  number. The app measures it from the ceiling down to the lowest point of the beam, the bottom of
  the soffit. So with a 5-1/2" drop and #9554, the fascias come out 4-3/4" wide and the soffit
  hangs the last 3/4". If you'd rather type the fascia width and have the soffit hang below that
  (a 5-1/2" fascia makes a 6-1/4" drop), say so.
- **Blocking spacing inside beaded beams.** Set at about 16" apart. You can change it under Beam
  construction details.
- **Labor per beam foot.** The quote guesses 0.3 hours per foot of beam in the shop and 0.3 to install
  (Quote → Standards). These need your real numbers.
- **Perimeter soffit lined up with full-height cabinets.** The "To cabinets" button on each wall
  puts the perimeter fascia face flush with the front of the face frame. Tell me if it should
  stand proud of it or sit back.

## Cabinets

- **Appliance clearances.** Built-in microwave and fireplace openings use the clearances typed for
  each appliance; the defaults are placeholders until you have the model's spec sheet.
